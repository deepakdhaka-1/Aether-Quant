# Name and logo proposal

## Recommended working candidate: Rulynx

**Pronunciation:** ROO-links

**Name idea:** “Rule” + “lynx”: inspectable trading rules with a sharp, observant market view.

**Descriptor:** Systematic markets

**Tagline:** Rules you can read. Markets you can trust.

The name is short, pronounceable, and less descriptive than “Aether Quant,” which may help it stand apart in search. Keep the descriptor near the name initially so people understand what the product does.

## Logo concept

The editable mark at `web/public/brand/rulynx-mark.svg` combines an R monogram with a rising signal line. Ink forms the stable structure; teal and mint show the market signal. It is original artwork drafted for this proposal and has not been checked through a comprehensive image or trademark search.

Use the mark with a plain “Rulynx” wordmark in Inter semibold. Keep the descriptor in regular Inter. Preserve the original colors and proportions; do not add exchange or asset logos to the brand mark.

## Preliminary name search

Checked on **16 October 2026** using exact-name public web searches for “Rulynx,” “Rulynx Quant,” and “Rulynx trading.” The results showed a profile for “Rulynx Agro Products” and unrelated usernames; no quant or trading product appeared in those results. This is a limited web screen, not proof of availability. [Search result: Rulynx Agro Products](https://www.blogger.com/profile/08141361605638856766)

The USPTO and WIPO interactive databases did not return a reliable candidate-specific result set during this pass. Domain registration, company registries, app stores, social handles, phonetic equivalents, and relevant Nice classes have not been cleared. Treat Rulynx as a **candidate for professional clearance**, not an approved or available mark.

## Why move away from Aether Quant?

The previous search found the exact name used publicly in quantitative trading and finance software, including [aetherquant.ai](https://www.aetherquant.ai/), an [MQL5 trading product](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/product/193252), and a [PyPI quantitative software package](https://pypi.org/project/aether-quant/). Ownership of the website is unknown in this review. These overlaps make a distinct name worth considering.

This is a proposal only. The live Aether Quant wordmark and app identity have not been renamed. Once the name is cleared and selected, update the application, domains, social accounts, legal pages, and release materials together.
